# Netflix-Content-Analysis
##Analysis of Netflix's content library to measure content growth, country contribution, and audience targeting using Excel and Tableau. The dataset was first cleaned in Excel and validated before the analysis and visualizations were made.
The goal of this project was to understand how Netflix's content changed over time and what trends emerged from its content library.

##Tools Used: 
-Excel
-Tableau

##Business Questions: 
1. Has Netflix shifted towards TV shows?
2. Which countries contribute the most content?
3. What content strategy does Netflix appear to prioritize?

##Data Cleaning and Validation: 
-Checked for duplicates
-Reported and replaced missing director, cast, and country values with "Unknown"
-Corrected Rating and Duration issues
-Standardized dates for consistency
-Investigated numerical values in the Title column and verified entries represent legitimate movie and show titles

##Findings: 
###Content Composition
-Movies represent the majority of Netflix's content at approximately 70%, indicating that films remain dominant
###Content Growth
-Both movies and TV shows surged between 2016 and 2020, with a peak in 2019
###Content Contribution
-The United States contributes the most content with nearly 3,000 entries, with India coming in second
###Audience Targeting
-Content ratings show mature audiences are favored, with TV-MA being the most common and TV-14 being second

##Recommendations: 
-Continue to monitor growth in Netflix's catalog, specifically the growth of TV shows, since analysis displayed notable growth in 2019
-Explore content expansion in more global markets, particularly high-contributing countries like India
-Continue analysis using information about audience engagement and viewership counts to understand content performance better

##Limitations: 
This dataset only provides information on what type of content is being published into the Netflix catalog, so it doesn't include measures of how customers respond to the content, like: 
-Viewership Counts
-Customer Ratings
-Watch Time
-Audience Engagement
-Revenue Metrics
-Subscription Growth
Thus, this project is unable to analyze what type of content Netflix should invest more in or which content strategy is the most successful.
